# Chart Selection Guide

## Bar Chart
- **Use When:** Compare categories (e.g., sales by region, counts by group)
- **Columns:** 1 categorical (x), 1 numerical (y)
- **Max Categories:** ~20 for readability

## Line Chart
- **Use When:** Show trends over time or ordered categories
- **Columns:** 1 ordinal (x), 1 numerical (y)
- **Best For:** Time series, cumulative data

## Scatter Plot
- **Use When:** Show relationship between two numerical variables
- **Columns:** 2 numerical (x, y)
- **Best For:** Correlation detection, outlier spotting

## Pie Chart
- **Use When:** Show proportions of a whole (<6 categories)
- **Columns:** 1 categorical (labels), 1 numerical (values)
- **Warning:** Avoid if categories > 6; use bar chart instead

## Box Plot
- **Use When:** Compare distributions across groups; detect outliers
- **Columns:** Multiple numerical columns
- **Best For:** Statistical summary visualization
